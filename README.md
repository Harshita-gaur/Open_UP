# Open_UP
Welcome to Open_Up, the social media platform where friendships and creativity thrive!  

At Open_Up, you can:  

**Follow Friends: **  Stay connected with your close ones and discover what they’re sharing.  
**Upload Pictures: **  Share moments that matter with your friends through stunning photos.  
**Like Pictures:**   Show your love and appreciation by liking the photos shared by your friends.  


Technologies Used:
**Backend:** Django (Python)  
**Frontend:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript  
**Database: **SQLite
